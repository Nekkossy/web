How to Care for Your Hello Kitty Tee

To keep your Hello Kitty t-shirt looking as adorable as the day you bought it, simply follow a few easy care tips! Machine wash it in cold water to maintain the fabric's softness and vibrant colors. Avoid using bleach to preserve the design, and for the best results, tumble dry on low heat or air dry. With the right care, your Hello Kitty t-shirt will stay looking cute and fresh for many seasons ahead!
